# SwiftDrop live demo accounts

Live system: **https://swiftdrop.bitcraft.biz** (API: `https://api.swiftdrop.bitcraft.biz`).

Every seeded account below uses the password **`Password@123`**. The data is synthetic
(generated by [`scripts/seed`](../../scripts/seed/README.md)): names, phone numbers and
`@example.com` e-mail addresses are not real people.

> [!NOTE]
> The **system administrator** account is not listed here. Its password is shared privately
> with assessors, because that account can manage every user and merchant.

## Quick start: one account per role

| Role | Username | What to try |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatcher | `dispatcher.colombo` | Unassigned queue for Colombo, assign or reassign couriers, courier workload |
| Courier | `courier.colombo.1` | Today's deliveries, pickup → in transit → out for delivery, photo proof of delivery, failure reasons |
| Customer | `fazil.hameed` | Book a parcel (live pricing), track it, in-app notifications, AI assistant |
| Merchant (approved) | `merchant.kandy-valley-spice` | Business dashboard, API keys, B2B orders, CSV upload |

Every seeded merchant is approved and every seeded courier is ready to work: their e-mail addresses
are not real, so invite and decision e-mails could not be read. To show those flows, use real
addresses: sign up a business at `/register/business` and approve it as that district's dispatcher
(**Merchant applications**), and register a courier as a dispatcher (**Couriers**) to send an invite.

## Notes

- Seeded on 19 Nov 2026: 238 parcels (198 customer bookings, 40 B2B), 139 delivered with
  photo proof, 17 failed with a reason, 17 cancelled; history spread over 23 Oct to 19 Nov.
- Accounts were created by the administrator, so they are e-mail-verified and need no code.
  New accounts registered on the site receive a real verification e-mail.
- If the data is reset (`scripts/aks/resume.sh --no-restore`, then a reseed with the same
  `SEED_PASSWORD`), the same usernames come back.
