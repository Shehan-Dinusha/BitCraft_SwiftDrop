# Proof-of-delivery photos for the seeder

Drop 6–8 photos of parcels at doorsteps here. The seeder uploads them as couriers'
proof-of-delivery photos, reusing them across all delivered parcels.

- Format: `.jpg`, `.jpeg` or `.png` (other files, like this README, are ignored)
- Size: under 5 MB each (the API's limit); under 300 KB is better, as they are uploaded ~100 times
- File names: anything; there is no naming rule. `pod-1.jpg`, `pod-2.jpg` ... keeps it tidy
- Content: photos your team took, so there is no licensing question. Avoid faces, house
  numbers and car plates; these images may be shown in the demo

With no photos here, the seeder generates simple placeholder images instead.
